About TimberCraft Carpentry

TimberCraft Carpentry is a small carpentry business based in Johannesburg. The business was created to provide practical and affordable woodworking solutions for homes, offices and small businesses.

TimberCraft focuses on quality workmanship and customised wooden products. Our services include custom furniture, cabinetry, shelving and wood repairs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality carpentry solutions through reliable workmanship, practical designs and friendly customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local carpentry business known for quality workmanship, dependable service and customer satisfaction.

Who We Serve

TimberCraft Carpentry serves homeowners, small businesses, offices and customers looking for customised wooden furniture and practical storage solutions.
