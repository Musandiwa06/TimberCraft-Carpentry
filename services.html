Our Services

TimberCraft Carpentry provides quality woodworking and carpentry services for homes, offices and small businesses in Johannesburg.

We provide customised solutions based on each customer's needs, space and budget.

Custom Furniture

We design and create customised wooden furniture including tables, desks, shelves, benches and other furniture according to the customer's requirements.

Starting price: R1,200

Cabinetry

We provide practical storage solutions for kitchens, bedrooms, offices and other spaces. Our cabinetry can be designed to suit the available space.

Starting price: R2,500

Shelving

We create wall-mounted and freestanding wooden shelves for homes, offices and businesses.

Starting price: R750

Wood Repairs

We repair damaged wooden furniture and surfaces. Our repair service helps customers restore useful wooden items instead of replacing them.

Starting price: R450

Door and Frame Work

We provide selected wooden door and frame repairs to improve the appearance and functionality of damaged or worn wooden structures.

Starting price: R900

Wood Refinishing

Our refinishing service includes sanding, surface preparation and refinishing to improve the appearance of wooden furniture and other wooden surfaces.

Starting price: R650
